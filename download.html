What you need

  • 64-bit Windows 10 (version 2004 or newer) or Windows 11.
  • An Intel, NVIDIA or AMD graphics chip with a video encoder (Quick Sync, NVENC or AMF).
  • About 100 MB of extra downloads during setup for the editor's video tools, and about 24 MB for the voice engine, unless you use the zip.

Windows says “protected your PC”

The installer isn't code-signed: a code-signing certificate costs hundreds of dollars every year, which is a lot for a free hobby project, so Windows SmartScreen doesn't recognise Archy yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. Only download Archy from this site or its GitHub releases. The full source is public, so you can read it or build it yourself.

What happens next

Archy starts in your system tray. Follow the how-to guide to pick your mic, calibrate voice clipping and save your first clip. Something wrong? Troubleshooting has the fixes.
