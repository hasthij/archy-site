How to clip your games

From download to your first clip in about five minutes. Everything here is free and runs on your PC.

InstallFirst-time setupSave a clipGames and captureQuality and sizeEdit and shareVoice tips

01Install Archy

  1. Get Archy-Setup.exe and run it. It installs just for you, so it doesn't need admin rights.
  2. Setup downloads the voice engine (about 24 MB) and the editor's video tools (FFmpeg, about 100 MB). It can look paused for a minute or two.
  3. If Windows is missing something Archy needs, Setup tells you and opens the right page. Archy never changes Windows settings on its own.
  4. Archy starts in the system tray, bottom-right next to the clock. Look for the blue A. A red dot on the icon means it's recording a game.

Windows SmartScreen warning? The installer isn't code-signed (a certificate costs hundreds of dollars a year). Click More info, then Run anyway. Blocked outright? Use the zip or portable version.

You need 64-bit Windows 10 (version 2004 or newer) or Windows 11.

02First-time setup

Open Archy from the Start menu or the tray icon, then go through these four things. It takes about two minutes.

Pick your microphone

Audio → Microphone. Choose the mic you actually talk into. Archy records your voice from it, and voice clipping listens to it too.

Calibrate voice clipping

Audio → Voice clipping → Calibrate to your voice → Calibrate. Everyone says things differently, and calibration teaches Archy how you say your phrase.

  1. When Archy shows “Say: Archer clip that”, say it the way you would mid-game: all in one go, normal speed, no careful pauses. It asks 5 times.
  2. When it shows “Now just talk normally”, chat or play for 15 seconds without saying the phrase. This is how Archy checks that it won't clip by accident.
  3. Wait about 30 seconds while it learns. It then shows something like “Caught 5 of 5” and switches Sensitivity to Calibrated.

Calibrate when you're not in the middle of a match: the learning step uses a few CPU cores for about 30 seconds. Calibrate again if you change your mic, your phrase, or where you sit. The recording never leaves your PC and is thrown away afterwards.

Add your games

Games. Roblox is already in the list. Use the running-apps picker to add anything else that's open. Other fullscreen apps (on by default) also records most other games running fullscreen.

Check your clips folder

Storage. Clips go to Videos\Archy by default. Click to choose a different folder.

03Save a clip

Open a game and play. Archy keeps the last moments in memory while a game is in front, and records nothing when no game is running. When something happens:

HowWhat gets saved
F8the last 30 seconds
Shift + F8the last 60 seconds
“Archer, clip that”the last 30 seconds
“Archer, clip the last minute”the last 60 seconds
Tray menupause or resume recording

You can change every hotkey, every phrase and the clip lengths in Archy. A chime tells you a clip was saved, and each new clip is also copied, so you can paste it straight into Discord.

04Games and capture

  • Window captures only the game, so popups never appear. Screen captures the whole monitor, for games that can't be captured as a window (pick which screen, like a screenshare).
  • Capture method (top bar): Auto uses Desktop Duplication for exclusive-fullscreen games (Minecraft and the like) and Windows capture for everything else. You can force either one.
  • If the cursor is missing in a clip, or a game's clip freezes, switch the capture method. Archy also suggests it.
  • Archy never injects into a game, so it is safe to run alongside anti-cheat.
  • Efficiency mode (on by default) keeps Archy on efficiency cores at low priority, so your game keeps the fast ones. On integrated graphics, raise Recorder GPU priority if clips look choppy.

05Quality and size

  • Quality: Discord (fits the 10 MB limit), or 360p up to 1440p. 720p is the recommended balance, about 19 MB per 30 seconds.
  • Frame rate: 24 to 144 fps. 60 is recommended; lower is less work for weak PCs. 120 and 144 need a monitor that fast; if your graphics chip can't record that fast, Archy uses 60.
  • Format: H.264, HEVC or AV1 in MP4, encoded on your graphics chip's own video engine (Intel Quick Sync, NVIDIA NVENC or AMD AMF).
  • Audio tracks: turn each on or off, pick the speakers or headphones to record from, and set the audio bitrate. You get everything you heard with your mic mixed in, plus the game alone, Discord alone, your mic alone and other apps.

06Edit and share

  • Paste into Discord: every new clip is already copied. Too big for the free 10 MB limit? Use Discord copy on the clip for a version that fits, or set Quality → Discord (10 MB) so new clips always fit.
  • Open the editor to trim, split and rearrange parts, and add text, images, drawings, video or music. Balance each audio source's volume. Undo and redo are there when you need them.
  • Export an MP4, GIF or MP3, save it, or copy it to the clipboard.

Nothing is uploaded at any point. Your clips live in Videos\Archy as ordinary MP4 files until you share them yourself.

07Voice tips

  • Use real, everyday words. The voice model knows real English words, so made-up names and gamer tags can't be recognized. That's why the default is “Archer” and not “Archy”.
  • 3–4 words you wouldn't say by accident work best.
  • Changed a phrase? Calibrate again. What Archy learned belongs to the phrase you calibrated.
  • Missing you? Calibrate again, or try Loose sensitivity. Clipping by accident? Try Strict.
