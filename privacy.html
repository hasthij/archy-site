Privacy

Short version: this website and the Archy app don't collect anything about you. There's nothing to opt out of.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no ads, no third-party scripts.
  • Fonts are served from this site, not from Google Fonts or any other font service.
  • The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles the technical details of each request (such as your IP address) to deliver the page and protect it from abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Download buttons link to GitHub, which serves the installer. See GitHub's privacy statement.

The Archy app

  • No account. You never sign up or log in.
  • Nothing is uploaded. Your clips are saved on your PC, in your Videos folder, and stay there unless you share them yourself.
  • No telemetry. Archy doesn't send usage data, crash reports or statistics.
  • Voice clipping runs on your PC. Keyword spotting for “Archer, clip that” happens on-device; your voice isn't sent anywhere.
  • Update check (optional). The only network use is an update check against GitHub, and only if you let it. GitHub sees that request like any other visit.
  • Installer. When you install Archy, the installer downloads FFmpeg and the voice model.

Open source

Archy is open source under the GPL-3.0 licence, so you don't have to take our word for any of this: read the code on GitHub.

Questions

Open an issue on GitHub. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
