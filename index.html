FAQ

Is it safe with anti-cheat?

Archy never injects anything into a game. It records with Windows Graphics Capture, which reads the game window from the outside, so it's safe to run alongside anti-cheat.

Where do my clips go?

Into your Videos folder, as ordinary MP4 files. They stay on your PC. Open one in Archy's editor to trim it, mix the audio, add text, images or music, then export an MP4 or GIF, or copy it to the clipboard.

Does it upload anything?

No. There's no account and nothing is uploaded; voice clipping runs on your PC too. The only network use is an optional update check against GitHub.

Which graphics cards does it work with?

Intel (Quick Sync), NVIDIA (NVENC) and AMD (AMF). Archy encodes on the graphics chip's own video engine, which is why the cost is small: measured in Roblox on an Intel iGPU, about 2–3% FPS.

What does the installer download?

The installer is about 12 MB. While it runs it also fetches FFmpeg and the voice model. You need Windows 10 version 2004 or newer, 64-bit.

Is it really free?

Yes. Archy is open source under the GPL-3.0 licence. No ads, no subscription, no account. The code is on GitHub.
