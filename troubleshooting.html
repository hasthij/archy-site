Troubleshooting

Most problems have a quick fix. If yours isn't here, Archy writes a log you can send us.

Voice clipping doesn't react

Try these in order:

  1. Calibrate. That fixes most cases: Audio → Voice clipping → Calibrate to your voice.
  2. Check Audio → Microphone is the mic you talk into.
  3. Check Windows mic access: Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone. Turn on Microphone access and Let desktop apps access your microphone.
  4. If Archy says the voice files are missing, run Archy-Setup.exe again to re-download them.
There's no tray icon after installing

Setup tells you if Archy didn't start. Antivirus is the usual cause: allow archy.exe in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Archy, then open Archy from the Start menu.

My voice isn't in the clip

Turn on Audio → Your microphone and check the right mic is picked. Your voice is mixed into track 1, so every player hears it.

The clip is too big for Discord

Click Discord copy on the clip in Archy, or set Quality → Discord (10 MB) so new clips always fit.

The cursor is missing, or a game's clip freezes

Switch the capture method in the top bar. Auto uses Desktop Duplication for exclusive-fullscreen games and Windows capture for everything else; forcing the other one often fixes it. Archy also suggests the switch.

Clips look choppy on integrated graphics

Raise Recorder GPU priority in settings (Equal is the default), or lower the frame rate or quality. 60 fps at 720p is the recommended balance.

Windows or my antivirus blocks the installer

The installer isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen may say “Windows protected your PC”: click More info, then Run anyway. If the exe is still blocked or deleted, use the zip: unzip it and run Archy-Setup.exe, or open the Portable folder and run archy.exe with no install. Only download Archy from its GitHub releases.

Where are my clips?

In Videos\Archy by default. Change it in Archy under Storage.

How do I stop Archy checking for updates?

Turn off General → Check for updates and Archy never goes online after install. Updates are only ever downloaded from github.com and always ask you before installing.

How do I uninstall it?

Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Archy → Uninstall. Your clips and settings are kept.

Still stuck?

Archy writes a log to %APPDATA%\Archy\archy.log. Please include it when you open an issue on GitHub.
